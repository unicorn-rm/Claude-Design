# Illustration & imagery (senior methodology)

Imagery sets the emotional tone faster than anything — and it's where "AI design"
screams: uncanny AI-stock illustration, meaningless gradient blobs, mismatched
clip-art. Senior imagery is **consistent, purposeful, and grounded** in real
sources. Follow `distinctive-design`; source real assets via `design-grounding`.

## Illustration

- **Choose a consistent real set** and style it to the brand: DrawKit, unDraw
  (recolor to your accent), Blush (composable), Open Doodles, Humaaans. One style,
  one palette — mixing illustration styles reads amateur.
- **Recolor to your tokens** (unDraw lets you set the accent) so illustrations
  belong to the system, not float in as stock.
- **Avoid obvious AI illustration** (melty hands, uncanny gradients, inconsistent
  style) — it's an instant credibility hit. Curated or commissioned-looking sets
  read intentional.

## Photography / art direction

- Direct for consistency: a coherent treatment (color grade, crop language, subject
  matter, light) so photos feel like one shoot, not a stock grab-bag.
- Duotone/overlay to tie photos to the palette; respect focal points in responsive
  crops (`<picture>` art direction — `responsive-design`).
- Real, specific images beat generic stock; if using stock, curate hard.

## Gradients, textures, noise (done well)

- **Warmth over flat gradients:** a subtle **grain/noise** overlay, a paper/film
  texture, or a soft duotone adds depth and defeats the "flat AI gradient" look.
  (Anthropic's warmth comes from paper + restraint, not blur.)
- **Gradients with intent:** two on-brand colors, soft, purposeful — not the default
  blue→purple mesh. Mesh gradients only if on-brand and subtle.
- **SVG backgrounds/patterns:** Hero Patterns, Haikei, or generative
  (`generative-and-creative-coding`) for signature texture.

## Meaning over decoration

Every image should do a job — show the product, set the mood, explain a concept.
Decorative-only blobs that mean nothing are the tell; cut or replace them.

## Reading it back

Reviewing imagery: one consistent illustration style recolored to brand? photos
art-directed to feel like one shoot? warmth/texture instead of flat gradient? no
AI-stock uncanny? does each image do a job? Name the weakest image and the grounded
fix. Generative texture → `generative-and-creative-coding`; color → `color-systems`.
