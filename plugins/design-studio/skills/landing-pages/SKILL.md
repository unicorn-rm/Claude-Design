# Landing pages (senior methodology)

A landing page has one job: get one visitor to take one action. It must be **clear
and persuasive AND distinctive** — most landings are generic stock skeletons in
blurple. Do both: conversion craft + `distinctive-design`, grounded in real landing
galleries via `design-grounding` (Land-book, Lapa, SaaSLandingPage, Awwwards).

## Message before decoration

- **One clear value proposition** above the fold: what it is, who it's for, why it's
  better — in plain, specific language (not "revolutionize your workflow"). The
  headline does the heavy lifting; `ux-principles` copy craft applies.
- **One primary action.** Everything points to it; secondary actions are visibly
  secondary. Multiple competing CTAs kill conversion.

## Structure around persuasion (then break the template)

The story, not the stock order: **hook (value) → how it works → proof → objection
handling → action**. Let the strongest part dominate; cut sections that don't move
the visitor. Reordering/removing the hero→3-cards→pricing→CTA skeleton is both a
conversion and an anti-slop win (`web-design` → section-patterns).

## Proof & trust

- Real social proof: specific testimonials with names/faces, real logos, concrete
  numbers with context — not three vague quotes. Handle the top objection explicitly.

## Craft that also converts

- Fast (performance is conversion): optimized images, lazy media, minimal blocking.
- Clear visual hierarchy so the eye reaches the action; real type and a specific
  accent draw attention where it matters. Mobile-first (`responsive-design`) — most
  landing traffic is mobile. Accessible (`accessibility`).

## Distinctive, not generic

Study **real high-craft landings** (Awwwards/Land-book) for structure to steal, and
avoid the category clichés (`design-research`). A landing can convert *and* have a
point of view — the best ones do both.

## Reading it back

Reviewing a landing: is the value clear above the fold? one dominant action? proof
specific and real? structure serving persuasion or running the stock skeleton?
distinctive or generic? fast + mobile-first + accessible? Name the biggest
conversion leak and the fix. Full site → `web-design`; ship it → `design-to-artifact`.
