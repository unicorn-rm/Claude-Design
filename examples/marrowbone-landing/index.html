Ponds, ditches, flooded quarries, and the things that only live in them

Nell Aitcheson spent six years walking the same eleven bodies of standing water in the Mendips, most of them small enough to miss from a road. This is what she found: 218 species, 41 plates drawn at the water's edge, and a method for reading a pond that takes about twenty minutes and no equipment.

It is a working book. The paper takes pencil, the binding opens flat on a knee, and the wrappers are printed letterpress so they wear in rather than out. We made 1 200 and that is all there will be.

Printed in September at Rathbone & Slee, Frome. Bound by hand in Bristol over three weeks.

“A pond is not a small lake. It is a different argument entirely, and it is usually winning.” Nell Aitcheson, p. 31
